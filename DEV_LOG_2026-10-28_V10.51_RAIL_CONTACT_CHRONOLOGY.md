# Pool World v10.51 — same-tick cushion/first-contact foul chronology
Date: 2026-10-28 (UTC). Development only. No release approval.

## Reproducible gameplay defect
A moving object ball can rebound from a cushion during the same 120 Hz physics tick in which the cue ball first contacts a legal target. The original loop resolved the cushion before calculating swept cue first contact. A physically *later* rail rebound was therefore missed by the no-rail/no-pot foul rule, incorrectly awarding ball-in-hand. This is a genuine match-rules defect, not a cosmetic optimization.

The deterministic browser fixture uses a legal solids contact at approximately t=0.08 of a tick and a separate moving stripe reaching a straight cushion at approximately t=0.15. The unchanged implementation recorded firstHit=1 and a real reflected rail velocity, but railAfterContact=false and an incorrect foul. Control fixtures at t=0.65 (rail before cue contact) and with no cue contact remain fouls.

## Fix
During the first movement/cushion pass, record the fractional time of actual straight-rail reflections whose first cue contact is not yet known. Once the existing swept first-contact solver establishes the earliest cue contact time, mark railAfterContact only for a genuine rebound later in that same tick. Earlier rebounds remain nonqualifying. Existing post-contact rail handling, physics impulses, restitution, shot-speed calibration, six pockets, and all four approved original audio data URIs remain unchanged. This correction is intentionally scoped to straight cushion chronology; jaw chronology is not newly asserted.
