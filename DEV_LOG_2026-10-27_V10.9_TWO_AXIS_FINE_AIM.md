# Pool World v10.9 — Two-axis near-cue fine aiming
Date: 2026-10-27 UTC
Scope: development/poolworld-match-engine ONLY. Production main/index.html not changed.

## Physical-device report and release status
Physical Samsung Galaxy A37 user reported shot power weak, ball movement jerky, and aiming difficult. No independent physical-device or human acceptance has occurred. This change addresses one reproducible cause of difficult aiming, NOT the remaining weak-shot or jerky-motion reports. Production release is blocked.

## Reproduced gameplay defect
When the player touches close to the cue ball, the fine-aim path used only horizontal pointer travel (clientX). With a horizontal cue, a purely vertical thumb drag of 40 CSS pixels left the cue angle unchanged (0 rad). The user could not steer a horizontal shot with a perpendicular vertical movement. A diagonal drag discarded its vertical component. The near-cue guard, existing horizontal 0.13 degree/CSS-pixel sensitivity, and direct distant-touch aiming must remain intact.

## Fix
- Capture aimStartY alongside aimStartX on pointerdown.
- For fine-aim and precision gestures, use (dx + dy*cos(aimStartAngle))*0.13 degrees/CSS pixel.
- Vertical thumb travel therefore adjusts a horizontal cue; travel along a vertical cue remains inert. Established horizontal drag sensitivity, cancelled-gesture restoration, pointerup commit, direct far-table pointing and cue-ball shot impulse remain unchanged.
- This is a targeted input mapping improvement, not a claim of physically optimal human ergonomics or measured Samsung latency.
