# Pool World v7.3 — Rail/jaw rolling distance correction (25 October 2026)

## Scope
Development-only improvement to the existing pool match engine. No changes to production, approved v3.9 audio, online play, monetisation, or expansion.

## Defect
The sphere's visual rolling quaternion was updated from the *proposed* unconstrained translation before the physics step resolved a rail clamp or pocket-jaw penetration. A ball that moved only 0.8 table units into a nearby rail could be rendered as though it had rolled almost 4 units, creating visible texture slip at hard rail contacts.

## Gameplay change
- `POOL_WORLD_6.0_STAGING_MATCH.html` commit `887d3518e3fbb10c4e6d1cb5508f56a5848d916a`.
- Defer `rotateRoll` until after cushion and jaw corrections in the per-ball physics step.
- Compute rotation from the resulting on-table displacement. Do not rotate potted balls.
- Preserve collision impulses, friction, shot strength, pocket capture, spin/follow, controls, and four approved v3.9 audio payloads.

## Required regression
- `tests/test_pool60_rail_roll.py` commit `4a5b562986c5da616c7a87686fdaa9697deffe65`.
- Workflow requirement commit `292bd8acc8ced9bc93108b347f1a8b3e205369c9`.
- 52/52 Chromium checks across 390×844, 844×390, 768×1024, and 1366×768; free rolling, left cushion, top cushion, corner jaw; actual-distance quaternion, finite normalized orientation, and legal in-bounds motion.
- During test development an existing retry-shot speed check intermittently failed because the browser could execute a physics tick between clicking SHOOT and sampling velocity. The test now dispatches the real button click and samples the speed synchronously (commit `fe4276c98c1cd876606f9ee72bbcda7facf943c3`). This fixes the timing assumption in the test, not shot-power physics.

## Limitations / next
Visual rotation now follows net constrained travel *before* object-object overlap corrections; the engine is still an approximate 2D model, and this is not validation against real filmed pool. No physical Samsung testing or independent human acceptance. Next work should stress random clustered layouts, shot feel, jaw throats, and complete matches. Never promote to production without real-device/human acceptance.
