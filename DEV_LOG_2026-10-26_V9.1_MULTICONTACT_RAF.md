# Pool World v9.1 — simultaneous-contact solver allocation rescue

Date: 26 October 2026 (UTC)
Branch: `development/poolworld-match-engine`
Status: candidate and development preview promoted and independently CI-verified; **NOT physically accepted on Samsung Galaxy A37**.

## Reproduced bottleneck
The coupled multi-ball collision solver in `resolveSimultaneousContacts` ran 32 Jacobi iterations and created a fresh `Array.map` impulses array on every iteration. It also recalculated unchanged pair-pair Jacobian influence coefficients on every iteration. This work occurs during clustered rack-break collisions and could contribute to frame-processing spikes.

Added `tests/test_pool60_multicontact_allocations.py` with 68 mandatory browser checks across four viewport sizes: 390×844, 844×390, 768×1024 and 1366×768. Three simultaneous-contact scenarios per viewport verify solver activation, low allocation count, finite state and non-increasing kinetic energy. The suite also measures **real headless Chromium requestAnimationFrame callbacks** for 1.4 seconds of an active full-rack break, recording callback gaps and frame CPU p50/p95/p99. These are diagnostic browser measurements, not Samsung display measurements.

## Code correction
Gameplay candidate commit `acc94a158a9b03a78869f2580fb611daaaf86606`, blob `0c68c934ffbc02e84c7af6972ffa12b4aa8022fa`:
- Replaced the 32 per-iteration impulses-array allocations with two reusable Float64Array buffers.
- Precomputed the pair-coupling coefficients once per contact batch rather than 32 times.
- Preserved Jacobi iteration order, double precision, blending, contact physics and energy limiting.
- Left cue power, pockets, controls, match rules, ball appearance and all four approved v3.9 audio data URIs unchanged.

The original source deterministically created 32 per-iteration `map` arrays; the new regression bounds all `Array.prototype.map` calls inside the simultaneous solver to six or fewer, including the two legitimate member-accumulation maps.
