## Reproduced defect
The previous `sound(type,intensity)` cloned an entire `HTMLAudioElement` on every accepted collision sound, even after a previous voice had finished. This produces recurring media element allocations during a match, beyond the v9.9 sound-event array allocation improvements. Recorded sounds, volume, pitch, event throttle and overlapping playback must be preserved.
