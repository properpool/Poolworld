# Pool World v6.7 — near-cue touchscreen aiming continuity

Date: 2026-10-25 (UTC)
Branch: `development/poolworld-match-engine`
Scope: core touch aiming only; no monetisation, marketing, multiplayer or expansion.

## Gameplay issue addressed
Previously, placing a finger on or close to the cue ball correctly avoided an immediate aim snap, but dragging the finger beyond the initial 20-CSS-pixel cue guard switched to absolute aiming mid-gesture. This could abruptly rotate the cue, particularly on compact Samsung portrait/landscape layouts.

## Change
- `POOL_WORLD_6.0_STAGING_MATCH.html`: detect touch/pen gestures beginning within the cue-ball guard, latch `aimFineDrag` for the whole pointer gesture, and apply relative 0.13°/CSS-pixel horizontal aiming instead of switching to absolute aiming mid-drag.
- Distant touch aiming remains absolute; the existing explicit precision mode remains supported.
- Pointer release clears `aimFineDrag`; pointer cancellation restores the original angle and clears the pointer state.
- No change to physics, power calibration, six-pocket geometry, table visuals or the four approved v3.9 sound data URIs.
