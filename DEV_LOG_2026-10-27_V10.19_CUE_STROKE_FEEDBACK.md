# Pool World v10.19 — visible cue pullback and short strike feedback

**Date:** 27 October 2026. **Scope:** development/poolworld-match-engine only. **Production:** untouched.

## Reproduced user-visible limitation
The original cue stick was drawn at a fixed position irrespective of selected shot power, and disappeared immediately when SHOOT was triggered. The selected impulse could be correct while the player received no visual indication of pullback or the cue's strike. The power UI also did not invalidate the aiming render, so a dynamic cue would not update live unless another input forced a redraw.

Mandatory new Chromium suite: `tests/test_pool60_cue_stroke_feedback.py`, registered in `.github/workflows/poolworld-staging-tests.yml`. The original implementation failed 8 of 12 checks across four viewports in workflow #414 (4/12), because the expected cue stroke was absent.

## Implementation
- The cue tip now retracts visibly with selected power, while preserving its existing wood shaft, ferrule and cue direction.
- A short 160ms strike animation remains visible briefly after a shot is triggered. The cue is rendered behind the moving balls and disappears after the animation; a browser stall cannot leave a frozen cue on the table.
- Changing power via the slider or touch pull marks the canvas dirty, updating the cue's pullback in the next animation frame.
- The cue shaft's CanvasGradient is cached once rather than allocated on each active shot frame.
- Cue animation is presentation-only: `shotSpeed`, initial cue velocity, fixed 120Hz physics, collisions, six pockets, turn/foul handling and all four approved v3.9 sound data URIs remain unchanged.
- Reset, retry and shot resolution clear the cue animation state.
