# Pool World v10.6 — full-width power thumb target and safe tap handling (27 October 2026)

## Reproduced user-facing defects
The visible POWER panel is wider than its narrow slider. On phone and landscape layouts, a thumb beginning a drag in the panel's side margins did nothing: the pointer handler was bound only to the track. The selected strength remained 45% even when the finger pulled to adjust it. This is an input hit-target defect, not a CPU optimisation.

A first candidate widened the touch target to the parent panel. Additional regression revealed that tapping the POWER label silently changed the chosen strength to 5%, and tapping PULL DOWN changed it to 100%. The final correction accepts side-margin drags only within the track's vertical bounds; labels and instructions remain inert.

## Release limitation and next priority
Chromium touch emulation and desktop pointer tests reproduce and fix a real control hit-target problem. They are **not** measured Samsung Galaxy A37 touch accuracy, frame rate, input-to-photon latency, shot feel, or independent human acceptance. Physical Samsung/human acceptance and explicit user approval remain mandatory before any production change. Next work should target meaningful remaining shot feel, motion and collision defects with reproducing browser tests, not speculative micro-optimisations. Preserve calibrated shot power, six-pocket physics, full matches, visuals and approved audio. No multiplayer, monetisation or expansion.
