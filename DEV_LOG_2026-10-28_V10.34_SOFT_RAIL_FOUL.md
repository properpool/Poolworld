# Pool World v10.34 — gentle cushion contact and foul adjudication
28 October 2026 · development branch only

## User-visible defect
A ball could genuinely rebound from a cushion after the cue legally hit an object ball, yet the shot could be called a foul if the rail collision was too quiet to trigger the sound effect. The old reflectCushion() combined a >0.06 normal-impact-speed audio threshold with the railAfterContact match-rule flag. Thus soft but real cushion contacts were ignored by the no-rail/no-pot foul rule.

## Gameplay correction
Decouple foul adjudication from sound volume. Any inward-moving physical cushion impulse occurring after first object contact sets shot.railAfterContact. Keep the existing >0.06 sound threshold and unchanged recorded audio, restitution, friction, spin, shotSpeed curve, pockets, rendering and turn-switching rules. A legal no-pot shot still passes to the other player; it must NOT grant ball-in-hand. A cushion struck before the first object contact does not satisfy the rule.
