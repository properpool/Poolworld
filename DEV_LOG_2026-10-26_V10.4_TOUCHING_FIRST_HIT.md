# Pool World v10.4 — phantom first-contact adjudication

Date: 26 October 2026. Branch: `development/poolworld-match-engine`.
**Production main/index.html is release-blocked and must remain untouched.**

## Reproduced gameplay defect

A cue ball can end a previous shot almost exactly touching another ball.
The pre-impact first-contact sweep previously set `shot.firstHit` immediately
for any starting separation at or below `2*rad`, even if the cue was moving
away, stationary, or sliding tangentially away and no object received an
impulse. This could incorrectly legalise a subsequent shot or hide the
actual first ball struck. It is a gameplay/rules defect, not a micro-optimisation.

Mandatory browser regression:
`tests/test_pool60_touching_first_hit.py` (204 checks, four viewports,
both horizontal directions, resting/away/tangential/near-tangential/
object-moving-away/closing/real-other-ball fixtures).
Baseline CI #315 (run 40420396027) reproduced **40 real phantom first-contact failures** in the original 172-check suite (132/172 passed).
CI #317 (run 40420574267) reproduced **8 additional near-tangential phantom first-contact failures**
on the intermediate closing-only candidate: 196/204 passed. All prior
registered suites passed on that run.

## Correction under validation

For a pair already inside the tiny contact tolerance at the start of a
120Hz tick, `earliestCueContact` no longer awards first contact from
proximity alone. The existing actual ball-impulse solver is responsible
for awarding first contact for an initially touching pair. The sweep
continues to detect genuinely new swept contacts from separated balls,
including v10.3's fast grazing collision repair.
