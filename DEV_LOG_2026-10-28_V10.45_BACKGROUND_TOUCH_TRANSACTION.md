# Pool World v10.45 — backgrounded touchscreen transaction safety

**Date:** 28 October 2026 (UTC)  
**Scope:** Development-only mobile aiming, power and ball-in-hand gesture safety.
**Production release remains blocked.**

## Reproduced gameplay defect

When the browser lost visibility while a touch was still captured, the
game had no explicit visibility/pagehide cleanup. If the browser did not
deliver a timely pointercancel, an unfinished power pull could retain a
changed shot strength, an aiming gesture could retain an unintended
direction, and an unfinished ball-in-hand drag could be confirmed by a
late release. More seriously, a two-finger pull-to-shoot gesture could
remain queued and **fire after the player returned to the game**.

In the 390×844 Chromium baseline, interrupting a 75% power pull left the
selected power at approximately **85.98%**. An interrupted queued
two-finger shot subsequently fired on the stale aiming finger's release.
These are reproducible browser-input scenarios, not measurements from
the user's physical Samsung Galaxy A37.

## Fix and promotion

The new `retireBackgroundedInput()` handler runs when the document
becomes hidden or the page is being left. It retires active aim and
power pointer capture, held fine-aim controls, ball-in-hand placement
and any queued two-finger shot. It restores the pre-gesture aim and
power and rolls back an unconfirmed cue-ball placement. Late releases
cannot commit an interrupted input transaction.

An **already committed moving shot is not modified** by this handler.
The existing 120Hz simulation, calibrated shot-speed curve, six-pocket
physics, complete match rules, visuals and four approved original v3.9
audio URI bytes are unchanged.

Exact passing staging blob `aee054cc5849613e0621f5981a0093c3f11d7140`
was promoted to development branch-root `index.html` in commit
`bef1fa12785ff40f38f30fa4a155ec413c69bec3`. The two files
were independently verified byte-identical.

**Production `main/index.html` was not modified**; last verified
blob `011657038a72ff255297ca78f595eef0e67df1e3`.

## Release limitation

Chromium pointer and synthetic visibility tests do not establish
physical Samsung Galaxy A37 FPS, touch responsiveness, shot strength,
ball smoothness, graphical acceptance or human approval. Physical
Samsung/human acceptance and explicit user approval remain mandatory
before any production deployment. No unfinished build should be
presented for user acceptance.
