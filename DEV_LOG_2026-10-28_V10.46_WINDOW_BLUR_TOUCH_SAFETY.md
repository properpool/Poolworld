# Pool World v10.46 — visible-window focus-loss touch safety

**Date:** 28 October 2026 (UTC). **Scope:** development-only mobile controls.
**Release status:** BLOCKED pending independent physical Samsung Galaxy A37 testing and human acceptance.

## Reproducible defect

The existing v10.45 handler correctly cancelled unfinished touches when
`visibilitychange` reported `hidden` or `pagehide` fired. However,
a mobile window can lose focus to browser chrome, the Android notification
shade or another UI while `document.visibilityState` remains `visible`.
A synthetic `window.blur` event did not retire captured pointers.

With a held aim, power, cue-ball placement or two-finger pull-to-shoot
gesture, a late release could commit unintended input. In the 390×844
Chromium fixture, a 75% selected shot was left at approximately 85.98%.
A queued two-finger shot also fired after focus loss and late release.

## Correction and promotion

The existing `retireBackgroundedInput()` is also bound to `window.blur`.
It cancels uncommitted aim/power/placement and queued shots, restores
the pre-gesture direction/strength/placement, and prevents stale releases
from mutating the match. Committed moving shots are not altered.

cannot measure actual Samsung Galaxy A37 FPS, touch latency, ball
