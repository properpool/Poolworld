## Changes

- Added twelve rounded cushion-nose (pocket jaw) collision points at the entrances to the four corner and two middle pockets. A ball striking the jaw rebounds and loses energy instead of unrealistically clipping through the cushion edge. Direct approaches to side and corner pockets remain pocketable.
- Added modest tangential energy loss on flat cushion bounces. Retained existing two-dimensional physics, existing 8-ball house rules, and all four byte-identical v3.9 recorded audio payloads.
- Expanded browser tests with eight explicit pocket-entry, jaw-rebound and energy-bound checks, and three full physical games with distinct break angles and powers. The natural-layout planner evaluates shots by restoring snapshots and then uses the actual SHOOT control, without moving balls between shots.

- The deterministic planner uses perfect state and physics simulation; this is not independent human gameplay or Samsung performance validation.
