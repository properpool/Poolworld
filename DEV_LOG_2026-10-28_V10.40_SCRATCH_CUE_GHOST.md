## Reproduced player-visible defect

When the cue ball was scratched and the shot settled in the same frame, `pocketBall()` created a 190ms sinking white-ball ghost. `resolveShot()` correctly respawned the cue for ball-in-hand immediately. The renderer then drew **both** the sinking ghost and the live white ball on the table at the same time. A quick drag or PLACE CUE confirmation could also leave the old ghost visible alongside the now-playable cue, producing a distracting double cue ball/teleport effect.

This is a real visual/interaction defect, not evidence of a change to ball speed, frame rate, physics or shot power.

## Corrected implementation

In the existing 190ms pocket-sink animation, suppress the *render-only* respawned cue and its ball-in-hand halo while a white-ball sink ghost exists. Once the sink finishes, the live cue appears in its legal ball-in-hand position. A new placement pointer gesture or PLACE CUE confirmation immediately retires the old scratch ghost so the cue becomes visible and controllable without delay.

Only presentation and cue-placement gesture cleanup changed. Authoritative ball positions, six-pocket capture, collision solver, shot impulse, spin, rules, scoring, full-match flow and audio recordings are unchanged.
