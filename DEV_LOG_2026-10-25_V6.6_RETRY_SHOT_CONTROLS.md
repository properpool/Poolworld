# Pool World v6.6 — Retry-shot control integrity
Date: 2026-10-25 UTC
Branch: development/poolworld-match-engine

## Concrete defect fixed
The RETRY SHOT button previously restored ball positions, turns and aim, but did not restore the shot's power, side spin or top/backspin. A power-handle release resets displayed power to 45%, so a replay could silently use a different shot from the original.

The snapshot now includes power, spin and follow. Restore reapplies those values to the engine and the visible power/side-spin/top-backspin sliders. The prior ball, turn, rule and aim restoration remains intact.
