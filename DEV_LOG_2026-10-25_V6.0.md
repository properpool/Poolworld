# Pool World v6.0 — critical shot power / pocket acceptance fix (staging candidate)

Date: 25 October 2026. Repo `properpool/Poolworld`, branch `development/poolworld-match-engine`. **NOT production.**

## User-reported severity / reproduced defect
A physical Samsung user reported that v5.9 was awful: shots had no power and balls would not pot. Reproduced a fundamental v5.9 gameplay defect in actual Chromium: the **default 45% break did not reach the racked balls**. At default power, the cue ball travelled only 322.5 table units from x=305.08 to x=627.60, while the rack begins at x=747.82; `shot.firstHit` stayed null. This made the first experience feel broken despite 157 previous passing tests. Six aligned pocket paths also needed real-browser end-to-end coverage.

## Changes in candidate
1. Recalibrated `shotSpeed(power, breakShot)` to `.45 + 7.9*(power/100)^.92` with a break factor of 1.17 (gameplay approximation, not measured real cue data). Normal 25/45/85% shot speeds now ~2.66/4.24/7.25 units per 60-Hz physics frame; default 45% break now **hits the rack** and settles.
2. Display live power percentage next to the power track. Existing deliberate downward pullback, fine aiming and approved audio remain unchanged.
3. Enlarged the playable table in 844×390 compact landscape from ~424px to ~471px width, while keeping table, SHOOT, track and controls visible without scrolling; also checked 780×360 and 740×340.
4. Added mobile Chromium end-to-end test: default 45% break makes contact, power percentage and downward drag work, three power settings are meaningfully distinct, and accurately aligned shots pot into **all six pockets** from realistic approach positions on both portrait and landscape viewport sizes.
5. Updated all 10 previous automated suites to point at v6.0; the full-match preview planner uses the exact same `shotSpeed()` as the actual SHOOT handler, avoiding false passes from an outdated duplicate formula.
6. Four approved v3.9 recorded sound payloads preserved byte-for-byte. Sound data block SHA-256 `9b33858d4c8c8f9d409f1c372e1cceafc6f422e678f0a4972794840718b47bcb` in both v5.9 and v6.0.
