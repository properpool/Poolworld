Date: 2026-10-25 (UTC). Branch: `development/poolworld-match-engine`.

## Gameplay changes
- Versioned candidate `POOL_WORLD_6.0_STAGING_MATCH.html`: the primary SHOOT/PLACE CUE button and laptop Space key now invoke the same `primaryAction()` path. When ball-in-hand is active, the first Space press confirms legal cue-ball placement without shooting; a second press shoots. Held Space repeat events cannot auto-fire.
- Stylus (`pointerType: 'pen'`) uses the same 20-CSS-pixel near-cue aiming guard as finger input, preventing unintended snaps while preserving deliberate distant aiming.
- The four approved v3.9 embedded sound sources were not modified.

## Limitations and next priority
All measurements are Chromium/browser scripted fixtures. The 144-shot stress run is **not** 144 human matches, and none of this establishes real Samsung, tablet stylus hardware or independent human playability. Next work should address real pocket-jaw near-miss tolerance, natural shot planning, physically believable roll and extended full-match stress. Do not promote to production before human/device acceptance.
