## Reproduced visual defect
The simulation advances at a fixed 120 Hz. The development preview previously drew the latest discrete physics state directly, causing irregular 1/3-step visual jumps even at nominal 60 Hz, 1/2-step jumps at 90 Hz, and intermittent zero-displacement frames at 144 Hz. This can look like jerky rolling even with short per-frame CPU times.

New mandatory browser regression: `tests/test_pool60_frame_interpolation.py`, introduced in commit `7c911315a585314dc3516a87a3ec45b3f4ca9111`, registered in GitHub Actions workflow commit `6750798c415741d50236a20602db62acde4064f4`.

Baseline workflow #233 (`40293685122`) FAILED 12 of 40 new checks, exactly the smooth-visual-movement checks at 60/90/144 Hz on each of four phone/tablet/desktop viewports. Recorded maximum relative step jitter: 60 Hz 0.6467; 90 Hz 0.6077; 144 Hz 1.0 (100%, including zero-motion frames). These are deterministic simulated requestAnimationFrame timestamps in headless Chromium, not physical display FPS measurements.

## Fix
Gameplay candidate commit `bdd1be6869db4a0b96d8a5556691bf83c8820b53`, blob `184c5f18537e0505628bc4fce3460bfc14e88a1b`:
- Interpolate only rendered ball positions between previous and current fixed-step physics states using the accumulator fraction.
- Restore authoritative ball coordinates immediately after each draw.
- Seed previous positions at the start of a shot, avoiding stale positions before the first physics tick.
- Keep all shot impulse, collision, pocket, turn, rules, spin and sound calculations on the original fixed-step physics state.

Candidate workflow #234 (`40293750063`) SUCCESS: 40/40 new checks and 5,341/5,341 checks total across 40 suites. All four approved v3.9 audio data URIs remain byte-identical.

Exact candidate blob promoted to development `index.html` in commit `4cd0f71bdcc395ed3da70387c6c8e83fc1551287`. Development index and candidate are blob-identical. No production changes.
