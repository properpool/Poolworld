# Pool World v7.4 — rail-side collision boundary guard (candidate, not yet promoted)

Date: 25 October 2026 UTC. Branch: `development/poolworld-match-engine`.

## Reproduced defect
A ball initially 0.3 table units inside a cushion could be displaced through the rail by the subsequent ball–ball overlap correction. The physics tick constrained the cushion *before* object contact, but never constrained it afterwards. The new mandatory `tests/test_pool60_post_contact_rail.py` reproduced all four cushion directions across four browser viewports: the pre-fix workflow run 40216746503 failed 16/52 checks, with 36/52 passing. Example: the left-rail ball ended at x=90.074346 while the legal centre minimum was L+rad=91.2.

## Candidate correction
Commit `15415ced2751a8e530377cce3cca4fd3a0a56428` refactors the existing cushion/pocket-mouth logic into `constrainRail(b)`, called both before and after object collision impulses. It does not broaden pocket capture or alter the four approved v3.9 sound data URIs. It aims to prevent rail-side clustered balls from briefly escaping the cloth after a contact correction.
