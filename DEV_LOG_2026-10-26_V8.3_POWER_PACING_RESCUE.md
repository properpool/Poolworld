# Pool World v8.3 — User-reported weak pull shots and low-FPS physics slowdown
Date: 2026-10-26 (UTC)

## Why this was necessary
The user reports that the **development preview**, not production, feels jerky and shots have almost no power on a physical Samsung Galaxy A37. Prior physics-only browser checks did not measure the reported interaction.

## Reproduced defects
1. The power handle used **absolute pointer Y** and auto-fired after a short downward movement of only ~28–32 CSS px. This could release a weak shot despite the user's intent to pull back for strength.
2. The rendering frame loop clamped elapsed time at 45 ms and processed at most six 120Hz physics steps per rendered frame. Under 15–20 FPS, simulation advanced slower than elapsed time, making ball movement artificially slow. New deterministic browser-frame regression measured **108/120** expected ticks at 20 FPS and **108/160** at 15 FPS before the fix. The first baseline workflow with mandatory tests, #200 (40259948842), failed; it also encountered a test-harness issue with a hidden settings control, subsequently corrected.

## Candidate correction
- Gameplay candidate commit `27fa0d4fc0afa8741940679663f0f1f3de2443ac` changes pullback to relative finger displacement, increases effective power on a deliberate pull, prevents short 32px drags from unexpectedly shooting, and retains tapping the track to select an exact strength. Deliberate pull or SHOOT button still fires.
- The physics frame loop now caps elapsed time at 83.3ms and processes up to eleven 120Hz steps per frame; at 15–20fps the new browser pacing regression meets elapsed-time simulation rather than falling behind.
- Existing pointer-capture regression caught a genuine candidate issue: a pointerup with zero displacement could overwrite an already selected power. The fix was committed as `a26e8c8efd93cc06a2d7daaaf2958bab00527cb6`.
- New mandatory browser test `tests/test_pool60_power_pacing.py` introduced by `e3adaab6f67e294f9d63d0caec25729caafd3b50`, workflow registration `7c566233eaa13dfc6a63272d17bec169d57f29fd`; test harness corrections `180f25ae84c744b1f41a8971816d7081751035f5` and `c1f6abc8b1f49008031e2ee2e3fa711beae76965`.
- The new 46 Chromium checks cover 15/20/30/60fps time pacing, actual cue-ball travel for 10/25/50/75/100% power on four form factors, touch 32px non-shot safety, and deliberate 63%-height pull generating a high-speed break.

## Important limits
These are browser-simulated touch and physics timing measurements, **not** actual Samsung frame-rate measurements or independent human acceptance. They demonstrate two real software defects corrected in the development preview, **not** that the game is now smooth or enjoyable on a physical device. Production release remains blocked pending independent physical-device/human acceptance and user approval. Next focus: measure actual browser frame-time variance, rendered ball travel at 30/60fps under full racks, input latency, and physical-device testing if available.
