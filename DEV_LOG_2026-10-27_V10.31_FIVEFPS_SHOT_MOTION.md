# Pool World v10.31 — sustained 5fps shot-motion time recovery (27 October 2026)

## User-visible defect and reproduction
At a sustained 5fps browser callback cadence (200ms), the old low-FPS detector discarded the slow-frame classification at 175ms. Each callback advanced at most eight 120Hz physics ticks although 24 were required. Over 24 callbacks the baseline executed 192 of the expected 576 ticks. Actual 85%-power cue travel was materially different from a fixed-step reference, making shots look weak and run in slow motion on severely overloaded devices.

This is a **synthetic Chromium cadence reproduction**, not evidence that the user's Samsung Galaxy A37 runs at 5fps or that physical device shot feel has improved.

## Mandatory regression
Added `tests/test_pool60_fivefps_motion.py` (84 checks across 360x800, 390x844, 844x390 and 768x1024) and registered it in `.github/workflows/poolworld-staging-tests.yml`. It covers sustained 5, 5.5, 6, 15 and 60fps tick pacing, debt, bounded per-frame work; isolated 180, 200 and 240ms hitches; actual 85%-power cue travel and velocity against a 480-tick physics reference; browser errors.

- Test-only #490 succeeded on the prior mandatory suites.
- Deliberately failing baseline #491 passed 60/84 new checks; the 24 failures reproduced the sustained 5/5.5fps slow-motion and actual shot-travel mismatch.
- Initial staging candidate #492 exposed a genuine existing 6fps budget regression: 92/96 prior sub-10fps checks, because floating-point rounding allowed 23 rather than 22 ticks. This candidate was NOT promoted.
- Corrected staging candidate #493 succeeded, 84/84 new checks, 96/96 sub-10fps checks, and all existing mandatory suites. Completed job and decoded logs inspected.

## Correction
After two consecutive slow frames, extend detection to 250ms and retain up to 500ms of simulation debt, with a maximum 28 ticks only for callbacks over 175ms. Preserve the previous 22-tick maximum for 6-9fps and the original eight-tick safety limit for isolated hitches. Physics step remains 1/120s. No changes to shotSpeed, cue impulses, collision response, pockets, match rules, graphics or audio.

Passing staging and promoted development branch-root `index.html` are identical Git blob `6300d2a47f67a1c1f0980a00c9393a8bd3f33e8d`. Promotion commit `18895556ef587c6d5b91d0122df47d2187b6fa80`.

Production `main/index.html` remains untouched, blob `011657038a72ff255297ca78f595eef0e67df1e3`. All four approved audio data URIs were compared byte-for-byte and remain unchanged.

## Release blocker
Physical Samsung Galaxy A37 FPS, input latency, actual shot feel and human acceptance are unverified. No production release or request to test unfinished builds. A real 5fps renderer will still look jerky; this fix prevents physics from also running in slow motion at that cadence.
