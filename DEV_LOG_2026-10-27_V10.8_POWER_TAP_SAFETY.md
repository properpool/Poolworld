## Reproduced user-visible weak-shot defect
After choosing a shot strength, a finger tap on the visible power handle or its enlarged side margins could unexpectedly change power. The old `padEnd` treated **every stationary tap** as a request to set absolute power from the finger's vertical position, even when the finger merely touched the handle or the larger panel. The visual handle uses a 22px-inset track mapping, while the tap shortcut uses a full-height mapping. The two disagree.

The new mandatory browser test `tests/test_pool60_power_tap_safety.py` checks 25%, 45% and 75% strength; handle, left-margin and right-margin taps; four portrait/landscape/tablet/desktop viewports; no accidental shots or stuck capture; unchanged actual `shotSpeed` impulse; and deliberate bare-track tap-to-select. It dispatches real Chromium touchscreen events on mobile-sized viewports and mouse events on desktop.

Baseline workflow #343 (ID 40440839382) **FAILED 80/116** new checks, including 36 genuine unintended-power changes. Examples: 390×844, selected 75% became 69.37% after tapping the handle or either margin; selected 25% became 31.78%. Test-file-only workflow #342 (ID 40440831541) passed all pre-existing suites.
