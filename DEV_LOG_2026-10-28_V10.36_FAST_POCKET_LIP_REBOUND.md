# Pool World v10.36 — receding fast pocket-lip rebound safety
28 October 2026 · development branch only

## Reproduced user-visible defect
At a side-pocket mouth, a moving ball whose trajectory was already receding from the actual pocket well could pass beyond the cushion while still travelling at normal speed. Once it crossed the narrow mouth tolerance, the engine snapped it back onto the rail, creating an abrupt ball-position jump and potentially delaying the real cushion rebound and its no-rail foul adjudication. This is a specific geometry/physics defect; it is not a measured Samsung A37 frame-rate problem.

## Fix and regression discipline
Added mandatory `tests/test_pool60_fast_lip_rebound.py` and registered it in `.github/workflows/poolworld-staging-tests.yml`. It checks 292 browser assertions across 360×800, 390×844, 844×390 and 768×1024. Both top and bottom side-pocket lips, both receding lateral directions, speeds 0.7/1.5/2.8, inward dissipative rebound, post-contact rail flag, no phantom pots, and real approaching pocket shots are covered.

They do NOT establish smoother overall moving-ball rendering, stronger-feeling shots, physical Samsung Galaxy A37 frame rate or touch latency, or independent human acceptance. Physical Samsung/human acceptance remains RELEASE-BLOCKING. Production must remain untouched without both independent physical/human acceptance and explicit user approval.
