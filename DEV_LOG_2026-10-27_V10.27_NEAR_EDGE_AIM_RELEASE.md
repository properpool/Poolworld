# Pool World v10.27 — near-edge coalesced precision aim release
Date: 27 October 2026 (UTC). Development branch only.

## User-visible defect
When a cue-side touchscreen fine-aim gesture or PRECISION-mode mouse drag
ended just outside the canvas, a browser could deliver the final movement
only in the captured pointerup event. The prior finishAim guard rejected
every off-canvas pointerup, even for relative fine aiming, so the final
finger position was lost. A real shot then followed the stale direction.
Direct absolute aiming intentionally still rejects off-cloth coordinates.

58 checks exercise captured cue-side touch and precision mouse release,
no intermediate pointermove, calibrated shot impulse/direction, pointer
retirement, no accidental shot, power preservation, distant off-screen
release rejection and browser exceptions.

## Release blockers

Galaxy A37 FPS, touch latency, visual smoothness, shot feel or human
acceptance. The user's weak-feeling shots, jerky movement and visual
quality concerns remain unresolved. Do not deploy to production without
independent physical-device/human acceptance and explicit user approval.
