# Pool World v7.5 — constrained rail-side overlap projection

Date: 2026-10-25 UTC. Development branch only. Production main/index.html was not modified.

## Reproduced defect
v7.4 correctly prevented a ball from escaping the cloth after a ball-ball impulse, but clamping its centre to the cushion could leave the contacting balls physically overlapping in the same frame. The new mandatory deterministic Chromium suite tests two- and three-ball rail-side contacts at rest and with an incoming velocity against all four cushions on four viewport sizes (390x844, 844x390, 768x1024, 1366x768). Before the correction, GitHub Actions run #161 (40221558266) failed 64 of the 196 new assertions: 132/196 PASS, with residual penetration as high as approximately 1.96 table units in the tested scenarios.

## Gameplay correction
Candidate gameplay commit 60209de0f0480664d7ff6540eb131f5207210770, file POOL_WORLD_6.0_STAGING_MATCH.html, Git blob efa364c861109b6b02df87fcfc56493a81a35c4e.

After the ordinary collision impulse and post-contact rail guard, `projectRailContactOverlaps()` performs up to 16 bounded, deterministic, ID-ordered position sweeps when contacts occurred. It allocates penetration correction according to available travel within the cloth boundary, so a ball held against a cushion cannot lose its outward half of the correction. This is a positional constraint only: it does not inject velocity, spin, extra shot energy or collision sounds. Ordinary pocket capture, jaw response, shot rules, v3.9 audio sources and main production remain untouched.

## Remaining limitations and next work
This is deterministic Chromium simulation, not a physical Samsung device test or independent human playability acceptance. The projection is an approximate positional solver and needs randomized multi-ball, high-speed and near-pocket-jaw stress; no real billiard footage calibration has been performed. Continue full-match stress, natural clustered layouts, finger aiming and premium table visuals. Do not modify main until real-device and human acceptance.
