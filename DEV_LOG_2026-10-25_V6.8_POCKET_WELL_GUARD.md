# Pool World v6.8 — pocket-well false-capture guard

Date: 2026-10-25 UTC. Scope: six-pocket physics and automated regression only.

## Concrete gameplay defect and fix

The previous physics loop could pocket an object ball solely because its centre crossed beyond the outer cushion within a broad mouth proximity band (1.2 × pocket radius). That band was wider than the actual circular pocket well. This caused false pots for grazing trajectories near the mouth even when the ball had not entered the well.

In `POOL_WORLD_6.0_STAGING_MATCH.html`, removed this broad overflow-to-pot shortcut. The existing circular pocket-well capture check remains authoritative. Balls passing the outer cushion beyond that capture area now receive a bounded cushion response instead of disappearing. Rounded jaw collision, six-pocket layout, calibrated shot power, cue/object motion and approved sound payloads remain otherwise unchanged.

## Limitations and next work

These are controlled Chromium fixtures, not measurements on a real Samsung phone, physical table validation, or independent human playability acceptance. The boundary fallback is a 2D approximation, not a full 3D pocket throat. Continue work on clustered layouts, uncertain finger aim, realistic jaw/rail energy transfer and full-match stress. Never promote to production without independent physical-device/human acceptance.
