# Pool World v10.42 — sideways-dominant diagonal power slip (28 October 2026)

## User-visible problem and reproduction
The narrow touchscreen power control correctly rejected purely sideways drags, but still treated a diagonal swipe with a dominant sideways component and a smaller 14–58px along-track component as an intentional power adjustment. On a 390×844 Chromium phone viewport, an accidental diagonal slip from a selected 75% could drop the power to 15.56% for a 58px upward wobble. The changed impulse would be used by SHOOT. This is a reproducible input problem relevant to the user's weak-shot complaint, **not** a measurement of the user's physical Samsung Galaxy A37.

## Correction and promotion
The power slider now rejects a gesture if its cross-axis displacement is at least 28px and more than 1.5 times its along-axis displacement. The selected power is restored during sideways-dominant movement and on final release (including coalesced release without an intermediate move). Intentional mostly-along-axis drags, absolute bare-track taps, the original calibrated shot-speed curve and the pull-to-shoot threshold remain unchanged.
