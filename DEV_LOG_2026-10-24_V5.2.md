## Remaining limitations and blockers

- **Not tested on a physical Samsung phone.** Mobile testing uses Chromium viewport emulation and pointer/touch events.
- **Not yet an independent, unscripted full-match acceptance test.** The eight-ball completion scenario used position-controlled fixtures.
- Physics are simplified 2D approximations; no realistic three-dimensional cue elevation, spin transfer, cloth simulation, or pocket-jaw modelling. Do not claim equivalence to Miniclip 8 Ball Pool.
- Local two-player only; online multiplayer, matchmaking, retention systems, and monetisation are absent.
- Simplified Pool World house rules (not a verified official tournament ruleset). Clarify break and foul rules before commercial release.
- Embedded audio attribution/licensing should be checked before commercial publication.
- CI workflow passing in GitHub is not established until a successful remote Actions run is observed. Local Playwright tests are the evidence currently available.
- Published game **remains unchanged** pending independent review, real-device testing, and explicit promotion.
