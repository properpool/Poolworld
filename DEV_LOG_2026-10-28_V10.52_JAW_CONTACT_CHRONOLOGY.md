# Pool World v10.52 — rounded pocket-jaw contact chronology (28 October 2026)

## User-visible defect
A legal cue-to-object contact followed by an unrelated object ball's **real rounded pocket-jaw rebound** inside the same 120Hz physics tick could be incorrectly adjudicated as a foul. The first jaw pass runs before the swept cue-contact chronology calculation; unlike the straight-cushion pass, it did not record the jaw impact's substep time. Consequently the player could wrongly lose their turn and award ball-in-hand to the opponent.

## Reproducible failing baseline
New mandatory `tests/test_pool60_jaw_contact_chronology.py` exercises four browser viewports (390x844, 844x390, 768x1024, 1366x768), both cue directions, 25%/75% shot power, and three chronology cases: cue before jaw, jaw before cue, and no cue contact. The jaw fixture rebounds from the upper-left corner rubber nose without reaching the straight rail. It checks the actual cue first hit, jaw rebound, chronological rail legality, resulting foul/ball-in-hand, turn, unchanged shot impulse, and finite physics state.

## Correction
During the first jaw pass, provide the previous position to `collideJaw` and calculate the swept sphere-to-rounded-nose entry fraction before positional projection. Record that fraction **only if an actual inward-velocity cushion rebound occurs** while the cue first-hit is still pending. The existing `earliestCueContact` chronology comparison now recognises a real jaw rebound after the cue's legal object contact. Existing straight-rail detection, post-contact jaw resolution, shot-power calibration, all six pockets, visuals and four approved v3.9 audio data URIs remain unchanged.
