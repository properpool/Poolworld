# Pool World development — v7.1 simultaneous multi-contact solver

Date: 25 October 2026 (UTC). Branch: `development/poolworld-match-engine`.
Scope: local core physics only. No production release or multiplayer/monetisation changes.

## Defect and remedy

v7.0 sorted collisions by swept contact time, eliminating dependence on rack-array order, but still resolved two truly simultaneous contacts sequentially. A central cue strike into a symmetric pair could impart different outgoing velocities to the two objects. v7.1 introduces a coupled normal-impulse solver for contacts whose swept times agree within 1e-7 of a physics step.

The solver assembles the pairwise normal-constraint coupling matrix, solves nonnegative impulses using 32 damped Jacobi iterations, accumulates corrections before application, samples tangential spin transfer from the same post-normal state, and spends cue follow/draw at most once for the contact group. It caps any numerical energy increase in a multi-contact group. Ordinary single impacts retain the previous collision path.

This is still an approximate 2D fixed-step rigid-ball model, **not** a validated continuous-contact billiards simulation. Its restitution and spin constants have not been calibrated against high-speed video of physical pool balls.
