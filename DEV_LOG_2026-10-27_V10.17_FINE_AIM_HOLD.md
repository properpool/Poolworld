# Pool World v10.17 — hold-to-repeat fine aiming (27 October 2026)

## User-visible problem
On touchscreens the two 0.5° fine-aim buttons previously advanced the cue only once per tap. Holding a button for 620ms still moved the cue by just 0.5°, requiring repeated separate taps to line up a shot. This was a reproducible control limitation, distinct from the unresolved Samsung A37 reports of weak shots, jerky ball motion and disappointing presentation.

## Reproduction and correction
- Added mandatory real Chromium mouse/touch regression tests in `tests/test_pool60_fine_aim_hold.py` and registered them in `.github/workflows/poolworld-staging-tests.yml`.
- On the unchanged gameplay code, workflow #401 failed the new suite at 16 of 80 checks: each of the eight viewport/input combinations failed both long-hold directions. The pre-existing suites remained successful on test-only workflow #400.
- Changed `POOL_WORLD_6.0_STAGING_MATCH.html` so a quick tap still adjusts precisely 0.5°, while a press held for 270ms starts a 0.5° adjustment every 70ms. Releasing, cancelling or losing pointer capture clears timers. The synthetic click after a held press is suppressed, preventing a stray extra 0.5°.
- Disabled long-press selection and context menus on the two aiming buttons. Keyboard Enter activation remains a single 0.5° step.
- Extended mandatory tests to cover mobile selection/context-menu safety, mouse and touchscreen holds in both directions, release stability, short taps, keyboard activation, unchanged selected power/shot impulse, and absence of browser errors.

## Release gates and limits
This is a Chromium browser control improvement, not independent physical Samsung Galaxy A37 acceptance. It does not establish smoother ball animation, stronger-feeling shots, or premium graphics. Production release remains blocked until independent physical-device/human acceptance and explicit user approval. No monetisation, multiplayer, expansion or production change is included.
