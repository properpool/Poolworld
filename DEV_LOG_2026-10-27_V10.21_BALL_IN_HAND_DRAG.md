# Pool World v10.21 — Foul cue-ball touch drag (27 October 2026)

## User-visible defect and reproduction
A player receiving ball in hand after a foul could tap a new position, but could **not** press, drag and release the cue ball to position it precisely. The old table `pointerdown` handler immediately set `ballInHand=false` at the touch-down coordinate; the subsequent movement was not a placement gesture. On portrait phone, BIG TABLE, landscape phone and tablet fixtures, a genuine Chromium touchscreen drag left the cue ball at its original location instead of the requested legal destination.

Mandatory regression: `tests/test_pool60_ball_in_hand_drag.py`, registered in `.github/workflows/poolworld-staging-tests.yml`. It uses real Chrome DevTools touchscreen events rather than only calling game functions. Four viewports: 360×800, 390×844, 844×390 and 768×1024. BIG TABLE is additionally checked on the supported portrait phone sizes (360×800 and 390×844).

## Fix
- Keep ball-in-hand active from pointerdown through drag, previewing legal cue-ball positions during the gesture.
- Commit the legal final position only on pointerup, preserving tap-to-place for existing users.
- Reject occupied, out-of-bounds and jaw/pocket-invalid drops; restore the original legal cue position instead of silently confirming a foul placement.
- On pointercancel or lostpointercapture, restore the original cue position, retain the incoming player's ball-in-hand turn and release the gesture.
- Preserve the existing rotated BIG TABLE coordinate mapping and avoid changes to aim, shot impulse, six-pocket physics, complete-match rules, rendering assets or approved audio.
