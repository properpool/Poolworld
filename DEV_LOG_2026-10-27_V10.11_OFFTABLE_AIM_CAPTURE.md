# Pool World v10.11 — captured off-table aim stability

Date: 2026-10-27 UTC. Development branch only: `development/poolworld-match-engine`.

## Reproduced user-visible defect

Direct cue aiming uses pointer capture. When a mouse or touchscreen drag
crossed the edge of the canvas, the browser continued delivering pointermove
events to the captured table. The previous `pointAim` implementation
accepted off-cloth coordinates, rotating the cue away from the last valid
in-table direction. The pointerup handler already ignored an outside release,
but it retained the wrong direction produced by the outside move.

At 390×844 in Chromium, a representative mouse drag established
0.299564787 radians inside the table and then changed to 0.131871309 radians
on an off-table right-edge move (approximately 9.61 degrees of unwanted
rotation). The same class of error reproduced at 844×390, 768×1024 and
1366×768, for mouse and real Chromium CDP touch input.

## Release limits

Chromium pointer-capture and CDP touch checks are NOT physical Samsung Galaxy
A37 performance, touchscreen-to-photon latency, user shot feel, or independent
human acceptance. Physical Samsung feedback still reports weak shot power,
jerky ball movement and difficult aiming. These remain RELEASE-BLOCKING.
No production promotion without independent physical/human acceptance and
explicit owner approval.

Next priorities: reproduce and correct weak shot feel and jerky motion on
representative phone hardware; avoid speculative power tuning or FPS claims.
