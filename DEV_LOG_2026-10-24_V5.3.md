## v5.3 code changes

1. **Fixed an eight-ball win-rule defect**: pocketing the final group ball and eight ball in the same shot no longer awards a win. Eligibility for the eight must exist **before the shot** (`shot.targetEight`). Legal eight-ball finishes remain wins.
2. Added two on-screen **0.5-degree fine-aim controls**, with touch/click support, to make precise aiming easier on narrow mobile screens. Fine aiming is blocked while balls move, on game over, or during unresolved ball-in-hand.
3. Preserved the exact four approved v3.9 embedded sound recordings (ball, cue, rail, pocket). Existing SHA-256 payload checks remain in the regression suite.
4. Added a **complete bot-planned natural-layout match** test: a real browser SHOOT click for each shot, physical simulation of the rack and all subsequent balls without between-shot fixture repositioning, and a deterministic search for legal shots. The bot evaluates hypothetical shot outcomes by snapshotting/restoring game state, then fires its selected shot through the real SHOOT control. A complete match finished in **37 physical shots**, Player 2 winning with the eight ball, leaving one stripe on the table. This is not human gameplay and is not an independent usability evaluation.
5. Added mobile viewport and touchscreen tests, precise-aim control checks, kinetic-energy bounds on head-on collisions, rule regression for simultaneous last-group/eight pocketing, and 90-frame emulated Chromium timing measurements.

## Limitations and next acceptance gates

- No physical Samsung handset tested, and no independent human player usability study.
- The bot has access to perfect game-state information and hypothetical physics simulations; its match result does not prove human players can finish matches easily.
- Realistic 3D spin transfer, cloth and pocket-jaw geometry are not implemented; existing physics remain simplified 2D. Graphical presentation still needs substantial improvement.
- Online multiplayer, matchmaking, accounts, progression, monetisation and commercial audio licensing clearance remain outstanding.
