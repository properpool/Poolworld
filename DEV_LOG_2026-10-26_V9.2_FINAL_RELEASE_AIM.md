# Pool World v9.2 — final-release aiming and shot direction

Date: 26 October 2026. Development branch: `development/poolworld-match-engine`. Production `main/index.html` is protected and not changed.

## Reproduced user-visible defect

On a rapid canvas aim gesture, Chromium may deliver `pointerdown` followed by `pointerup` without a `pointermove`. The previous `finishAim` cleared pointer capture without applying the final release coordinates, so the displayed cue angle and next shot used the start position rather than where the player released.

New mandatory regression: `tests/test_pool60_final_release_aim.py` (commit `c8ff136c173b4123b528caef914cf628ce519833`), registered in `.github/workflows/poolworld-staging-tests.yml` (commit `bc4b035cd13982e0aac09f4e92a237e380e65a78`). Four Chromium viewports: 390×844, 844×390, 768×1024, 1366×768. Browser-driven mouse press, touch start, coalesced synthetic final release, shot direction, repaint, off-table release and capture cleanup.

Baseline CI #248 (run 40315977274) **FAILED 28/44 checks**; 16 actual failures (four per viewport): wrong final absolute aim, wrong resulting shot direction, missing final fine-aim movement and wrong resulting touch-shot direction. Example 390×844: absolute aim remained −0.56960 radians instead of the release angle +0.19987; fine aim remained 1.04720 instead of 1.21056 radians. Baseline CI #247 (test-file-only) succeeded on the previous 42 suites.

## Gameplay correction

Candidate gameplay commit `5fbd027cf31e56857527ed7e109ea71f9e97e4b9`, blob `6dd36410a835b121707881bba4bff3f5b137c706`. On `pointerup`, before clearing pointer capture, `finishAim` applies `pointAim(e)` using the final release position **only when inside the canvas bounds**. This handles coalesced movement in both absolute mouse aiming and relative touch fine aiming; off-table releases preserve the last intentional angle. Pointer cancellation and lost capture still restore the pre-gesture angle and never shoot.

Independent development-preview CI #250 (run 40316535889) **SUCCESS 5,509/5,509 across 43 suites**, new 44/44, all four sounds PASS. Workflow jobs and decoded logs inspected.

## Acceptance limits and next priorities

The automated tests use Chromium and a synthetic final-release event to reproduce a mobile event-coalescing pattern. They **do not** constitute independent physical Samsung Galaxy A37 testing, actual device GPU/frame-rate measurement or human gameplay acceptance. The user's reported weak/jerky physical gameplay remains release-blocking until independently verified. Next work: real-browser active full-rack frame-time p95/p99 and input-to-visible latency, touch responsiveness under dropped/coalesced events, and measurable physics/render bottlenecks. Preserve calibrated power, six-pocket rules, match completion, premium visuals and all four approved audio sources. No monetisation, multiplayer, expansion or production promotion.
