# Pool World v7.7 — cue ball-in-hand jaw clearance
Date: 2026-10-25 (UTC)

## Defect reproduced
Ball-in-hand `canPlace(x,y)` excluded the six pocket wells and other object balls but did not exclude the twelve physical rubber jaw noses. A player could tap a spot on the cloth with the cue ball overlapping a jaw; the placement was accepted and a subsequent physics tick could abruptly displace the ball. The same invalid position could be silently accepted by PLACE CUE.

## Fix
`POOL_WORLD_6.0_STAGING_MATCH.html` now checks `jaws.every(([jx,jy])=>Math.hypot(x-jx,y-jy)>=rad+jawRadius+.1)` in the shared `canPlace` function. This is the same geometric jaw radius used for collisions, plus a 0.1-unit safety clearance. It applies to direct ball-in-hand placement and the confirm/reposition path. Normal shots, pocket capture, audio, and collision impulses were not changed.
