# Pool World v10.35 — low-speed pocket-lip stall recovery
28 October 2026 · development branch only

## Reproduced user-visible gameplay defect
A slowly rolling object ball could cross the open lip beside a side pocket, miss the actual circular pocket well, and stop permanently with its centre beyond the playable cloth. It was neither potted nor rebounded. The next player could be left with an unreachable ball; after an otherwise legal first object contact, the missing rebound could also produce a false no-rail/no-pot foul. The failure was reproducible with 0.14 and 0.22 unit/tick-fixture velocities near both side-pocket lips, left and right offsets, on four Chromium viewports.

## Correction
Keep the six-pocket circular capture test authoritative. Preserve the previous open mouth and full-speed cut-pot paths. Only once a ball slows to 0.08 units or below while beyond the mouth edge, estimate its remaining 120Hz friction-limited straight-line coast. If that path can still reach a real pocket capture circle, leave the mouth open. Otherwise constrain the ball to the cloth and apply the normal dissipative cushion impulse. This prevents stationary off-cloth balls without inventing a pocket capture or changing the calibrated shotSpeed, restitution, spin, visuals or match rules. A real rebound after legal object contact still satisfies the no-rail/no-pot rule.
