# Pool World v8.0 — Interrupted pointer-capture recovery
Date: 2026-10-25 UTC
Branch: `development/poolworld-match-engine`

## Scope
Core input reliability only: Samsung-sized portrait/landscape touch, tablet touch/pen and laptop pointer aiming/power. No multiplayer, monetisation, marketing, expansion or production deployment.

## Reproduced defect
When the browser or operating system revoked `pointercapture` without dispatching `pointerup` on the table/slider, `aimPointer` or `powerPointer` could remain latched. Subsequent aim drags or power pulls were ignored until a rack reset/reload. Interrupted power gestures could also leave an unintended intermediate setting. This is a browser event-handling defect; it is not evidence of a specific physical Samsung failure.

## Implementation
- The table listens for `lostpointercapture` and routes it to `finishAim`.
- A lost-capture event restores the pre-gesture aim and clears `aimPointer`, `dragAim` and `aimFineDrag`; normal completed `pointerup` preserves the accepted angle.
- The power slider listens for `lostpointercapture` and routes it to `padEnd`.
- A lost-capture event restores the pre-drag power, clears `powerPointer` and `powerDrag`, and cannot fire a shot; normal completed pointerup retains the chosen power.
- Events for an unrelated pointer ID do not cancel the active gesture.
- The four approved v3.9 audio data URIs remain byte-identical.
