# Pool World v10.56 — swept rounded pocket-jaw grazing collisions
Date: 28 October 2026 (UTC)
Branch: `development/poolworld-match-engine`
Production: NOT MODIFIED

## User-visible defect
At high shot speed, a ball could cross the outer edge of a rounded rubber pocket jaw between two 120Hz physics samples. Both endpoint positions remained outside the jaw's expanded contact circle, so the existing endpoint-only jaw solver ignored a real collision. The ball then continued straight through the pocket lip rather than taking a physical cushion deflection. This is a meaningful shot-outcome and collision-realism defect, not a micro-optimisation.

## Physics correction and staged verification
The first jaw pass now performs an analytic swept segment/circle entry check **only when the endpoint is outside the jaw**. When a real incoming contact occurred during the step, the engine applies the existing dissipative cushion impulse at the entry normal and integrates the remaining fraction of the 120Hz tick from that point. It records the true rail-contact fraction for same-tick first-hit chronology. Clear misses and the established endpoint-overlap and post-contact jaw passes retain their previous behaviour. The fixed physics time-step scale is passed explicitly. Shot power, six pockets, complete-match rules, table visuals and all four approved v3.9 audio URI bytes are unchanged.
