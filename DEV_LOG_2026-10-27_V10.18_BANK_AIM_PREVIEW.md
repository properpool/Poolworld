# Pool World v10.18 — one-cushion bank aiming preview
Date: 27 October 2026 (UTC)
Scope: development/poolworld-match-engine ONLY; production remains untouched.

## User-visible defect
The cue guide ended at the first cushion even when a legitimate bank shot was available.
Players had no indication of the approximate outgoing path after a bank, making
bank shots needlessly difficult on a narrow phone display.

## Correction
A one-cushion dashed turquoise preview appears when the initial cue ray
hits uninterrupted cushion rubber before any object ball. The first leg
remains the existing aim guide; the second uses the same 0.78 normal
restitution as the rail solver for a *neutral-spin approximation*.
The preview ends at the first intersected object ball, next cushion, or
220 table units, whichever comes first. It is suppressed through corner
pockets, side-pocket mouths and jaw-clearance zones, and when GUIDE is off.
It is explicitly an approximation: side spin can alter the real bank angle.
The correction does NOT modify cue-ball velocity, shot power, 120Hz physics,
six-pocket geometry, match rules, touchscreen capture, or approved audio.
