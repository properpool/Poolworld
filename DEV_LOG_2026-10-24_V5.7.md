## Substantive improvements
1. Added an explicit PRECISION toggle: touch-drag adjusts aim by 0.13 degrees per screen CSS pixel, with no angle snap on initial contact, preserving direct tap aiming and existing ±0.5-degree buttons. Pointer identity and cancellation handling prevent stale drags.
2. Made pullback safer: tapping the power track selects strength without firing; only a deliberate downward movement of at least 12 CSS pixels shoots. Upward movements do not fire; cancelled drags restore the previous power; after a successful pullback shot power resets to 45.
3. Compact short-screen landscape layout: the table, power track and SHOOT button are all visible within a 844×390 emulated viewport without vertical or horizontal scrolling.
4. All four approved v3.9 audio payload hashes unchanged (`ball` 6e0440053e5859b48fa1, `cue` 7e2b39137a6de6afd751, `rail` e405df7c5c3c3937a6e2, `pocket` 6931e106287f67f9b771). No changes to collision, pocket, spin or rules engine.
