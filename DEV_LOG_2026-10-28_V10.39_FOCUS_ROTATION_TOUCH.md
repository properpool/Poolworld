# Pool World v10.39 — BIG TABLE rotation touch-transaction safety

**Date:** 28 October 2026 UTC. **Branch:** `development/poolworld-match-engine`. **Production:** unchanged.

## Reproducible player-visible defect

On a portrait phone in BIG TABLE mode, rotate the viewport to landscape
while a pointer is still captured:

- **Aim:** the rotation was deferred during the drag, but releasing the finger
  left the sideways BIG TABLE overlay stranded in landscape.
- **Power:** the same stranded overlay occurred after a power-handle gesture.
- **Ball in hand:** the rotation immediately reparented the canvas while cue
  placement was active, invalidating its coordinate transform and cancelling
  the ongoing placement, which could visibly snap the cue ball back.

This is a touchscreen control defect, not an inference about Samsung frame
rate or the weak/jerky shot report.

## Mandatory regression evidence

`tests/test_pool60_focus_rotation_gesture.py` runs real Chromium mouse
pointer-down/up gestures and browser viewport rotations for aim, power and
cue-ball placement across **360×800, 390×844, 412×915 and 430×932**.
It checks ownership during rotation, cue-ball position stability, immediate
landscape recovery after release, no unintended shot or stuck pointer,
preserved 75% power, normal idle rotation and no JavaScript exceptions.
The workflow registers the new mandatory suite.

- Test-only run **#533** (ID `40635393138`) succeeded on the prior suites.
- Initial baseline run **#534** (ID `40635402268`) failed after its
  deliberately failing aim case because the first test harness lacked

- Corrected unchanged-gameplay baseline **#535** (ID `40635448025`)
  failed **16 of 72 checks** (**56/72 passed**), consistently across all four

- Corrected staging candidate **#536** (ID `40635468970`) succeeded

- Independent development-root promotion **#537** (ID `40636045933`)

The final statuses, workflow jobs and decoded job logs for these runs were
inspected. The #534 failure was a test-isolation defect; the 16 failures in
#535 are the genuine reproduced gameplay defects.

## Minimal correction

`setTableFocus` now refuses to reparent the canvas while a cue-ball
placement pointer is active, matching its existing protection for aim and
power drags. On `pointerup`, `pointercancel` or `lostpointercapture`,
a deferred orientation check restores the native landscape layout only
after **all** active aim, power and placement gestures have retired.

No shot impulse curve, cue/object physics, six-pocket geometry, match rules,
table rendering, or recorded audio was changed.

## Release block and next priority

These are Chromium browser pointer and resize tests, **not independent
physical Samsung Galaxy A37 testing**. Weak-feeling shots, jerky ball
movement, device FPS/latency and human acceptance remain unverified.
Independent physical-device testing and explicit user approval remain
mandatory before any production deployment. Next work should prioritise
a measurable defect in shot strength or moving-ball smoothness rather
than cosmetic or micro-optimisation changes.
