# Pool World v9.4 — jaw collision broad-phase (26 October 2026)

## Scope
Development-only performance rescue for `properpool/Poolworld`, branch `development/poolworld-match-engine`. User reports weak/jerky gameplay on a physical Samsung Galaxy A37. **No physical-device or human acceptance test has occurred.** This is not a release or a claim of improved Samsung frame rate.

## Reproduced bottleneck
The existing `collideJaw` loop checked all twelve rubber pocket noses on every invocation, even when a ball was safely in the centre of the cloth and geometrically unable to reach a jaw.

Mandatory browser regression: `tests/test_pool60_jaw_broadphase.py` (commit `43d7b01226926d424b4e92cb18475724809d9322`), registered in the workflow (commit `b66985e87b90a54dea5c0ef9f81a48b12022e980`).

Baseline CI **#258** (run `40332896879`) failed **8 of 48 checks** across 390×844, 844×390, 768×1024 and 1366×768. In each viewport:
- 168 interior `collideJaw` calls unnecessarily traversed the nose list 168 times.
- Over a 72-frame active full-rack break, instrumentation observed **2,682 jaw-list traversals**, including calls from the overlap projector, while `collideJaw` was invoked 2,416 times.
- All twelve jaw-contact fixtures still rebounded correctly. The baseline failure was unnecessary broad-phase work, not a physics failure.

## Fix
Candidate gameplay commit `65f214c22c41972f1aab65308990b7ef58f93253`, blob `bb1b56e382b4569ffdcf23f7bf3cf0dd6e62f8d3`. Added a conservative interior-rectangle early return to `collideJaw`: a ball whose centre is at least `rad+jawRadius` from every cushion edge cannot intersect any rubber nose, since all noses lie on those edges. All balls near a rail or jaw retain the exact original twelve-nose narrow-phase, normal impulse, spin and energy behaviour.

Across the same 72-frame break, jaw-list traversals fell from **2,682 to 266** (about **90.1% fewer**), with the same 2,416 `collideJaw` calls. All 168 interior fixture scans were eliminated. All twelve near-jaw rebounds remained finite and dissipative. This is a deterministic count reduction, **not** a controlled CPU, GPU, FPS, latency or physical-phone performance measurement.
