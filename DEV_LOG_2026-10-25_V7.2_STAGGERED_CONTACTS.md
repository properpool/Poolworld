# Pool World v7.2 — near-simultaneous clustered impacts with spin/draw

Date: 2026-10-25 UTC. Branch: `development/poolworld-match-engine`.

## Actual change

At 120Hz, two balls contacted within ~0.10 milliseconds of one another could previously be handled by two sequential impulses because the coupled collision solver required essentially exact swept-contact-time equality (<1e-7 of a timestep). The development candidate now groups contacts within 0.012 of a 1/120-second physics step (0.10ms), so near-coincident clustered hits receive a coupled response. More separated hits still follow chronological sequential resolution. This is a tuning approximation, **not** a measured real billiard contact duration or validated physical calibration.

## Acceptance limitations and next work

All tests are deterministic Chromium/browser fixtures. **No physical Samsung device, human playability, or real pool footage validation has occurred.** A 0.10ms grouping window is a modelling assumption. Investigate broader randomized clustered and high-spin shots, pocket-throat/jaw near misses, spin/follow transfer in staggered high-speed impacts, rolling realism and complete-match stress before any production promotion. Preserve approved audio. Do not promote production without independent real-device/human acceptance.
