# Pool World v10.61 — touching cue impact before later swept first hit
Date: 2026-10-29 UTC. Branch: `development/poolworld-match-engine`.

## User-visible defect reproduced
At 120 Hz, a cue ball already touching a solid or stripe can impart a real closing impulse at t=0 while also sweeping into a second, separate object later in the SAME physics tick. The geometric `earliestCueContact` prepass previously ignored the touching object, prematurely selected the later swept object as `shot.firstHit`, and the real impulse solver was unable to correct the already-assigned first hit. A legal first contact was wrongly called an illegal hit, awarding the opponent a foul and ball-in-hand.

## Mandatory reproduction
`tests/test_pool60_touching_vs_swept_first_hit.py` runs 3,460 checks across 390x844, 844x390, 768x1024 and 1366x768 Chromium viewports. Fixtures vary touching solid/stripe, shot direction, upper/lower glancing offset, three angles, three velocities and two later swept-hit gaps. They verify separate non-overlapping object balls, real impulses to BOTH balls, correct first hit, no false foul/ball-in-hand, and finite completed-shot state.

Test-only CI #668 succeeded with the pre-existing suites. Unchanged staging baseline CI #669 deliberately failed 1,152/3,460 new checks (2,308 passed), all attributable to wrong first-hit adjudication and consequent false ball-in-hand; its jobs and decoded logs were inspected.

## Repair and verification
In the staging candidate, a pre-touching cue/object pair defers the geometric prepass only if it remains overlapped at the endpoint AND has a closing normal relative velocity. The impulse solver then records the real t=0 hit ahead of later swept contacts. Separating and tangential touches remain ignored, preserving existing no-phantom-first-hit behaviour.
