## Physics changes
1. Removed nonphysical lateral curving of a free-rolling ball caused by side-spin. Side spin now decays while rolling, but acts during rail contacts.
2. Added signed, bounded cushion tangential impulses from side-spin. A right-rail hit with positive versus negative English now produces opposite lateral deflection; cushion contact consumes some spin. Impulse capped at 0.45 in engine velocity units.
3. Strengthened and slowed decay of cue-ball longitudinal follow/draw so a head-on draw can reverse the cue after object-ball impact, neutral stun mostly stops it, and follow continues forward. Cue post-impact spin impulse capped at 0.95 in engine velocity units. This is an arcade approximation; the spin-to-linear energy transfer is not a full rotational physics solver.
4. All four approved v3.9 embedded sound payloads remain byte-for-byte unchanged. No rules, menu, online, production or monetisation changes.
