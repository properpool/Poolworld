# Pool World v10.53 — cue collision before side-pocket capture (28 October 2026)

## User-visible defect
A legal cue/object impact could be erased if that object entered a side-pocket well **later in the same 120Hz physics tick**. The pocket sweep runs before the ball-ball impulse solver, so the moving object was marked potted and skipped during cue contact detection and impulse resolution. This incorrectly reported no legal first contact, awarded a foul and ball-in-hand, and left the cue ball moving as if it had missed entirely.

## Deterministic reproduction
New mandatory `tests/test_pool60_pocket_contact_order.py` exercises four Chromium viewports (390x844, 844x390, 768x1024, 1366x768), both approach directions, 75% and 90% calibrated power, and three cases: cue impact **before** side-pocket entry, pocket entry **before** possible cue impact, and an object moving **away** from the pocket. The fixture uses the upper-middle well and an object initially outside its capture circle. Assertions cover actual first hit, cue impulse, pot/escape, foul, turn, unchanged power and finite ball state.

## Correction
The pocket sweep now calculates the true swept well-entry time and the cue/object contact time within that 120Hz step. It defers **only** an object-ball pocket capture when a genuine approaching cue contact occurs earlier. The existing swept ball-ball solver then transfers the impulse and recognises the legal first hit; the post-collision pocket pass captures a ball still inside the well. Pockets entered before cue contact remain immediate, as do all unrelated pocket events.
