# Pool World — Room progression vision

Status: Approved creative direction; design proposal, not implemented gameplay.

## Core concept
Players begin in a worn, old-school pool back room with a basic table. Winning matches, improving skill and completing career challenges earns in-game rewards and unlocks room renovations and progressively grander venues. The player can walk around and explore rooms, meet NPCs and later online players, approach tables and transition into precise pool gameplay.

## Proposed progression (level thresholds provisional)
- Level 1 — The Old Back Room: dated wallpaper, worn carpet, basic lighting, old green-felt table.
- Level 5 — Renovated Games Room: improved flooring and lights, refreshed cloth, cue rack, first trophies.
- Level 15 — Sports Bar: big-screen TVs, dartboard, bar, several pool tables and spectators.
- Level 30 — Luxury Pool Lounge: premium interiors, exclusive opponents, advanced competitions.
- Level 50 — World Championship: tournament arena, crowd, trophy and dramatic presentation.

## Player agency
- Spend earned in-game currency on individual renovations, e.g. flooring, lights, table, furniture, jukebox and decor.
- Keep and revisit the original room as a persistent personal space.
- Do not require real-money purchases to progress.
- Cosmetic upgrades must not alter core competitive physics unfairly.

## Implementation strategy
1. Stabilise complete single-player 8-ball match first.
2. Add persistent profile, XP, coins and a basic upgrade catalogue.
3. Create a lightweight explorable starter room for mobile; walking to the table transitions into pool view.
4. Expand room themes and AI characters; optimise for Android.
5. Add social/multiplayer functionality only after reliable testing and sustainable infrastructure.

## Acceptance criteria for initial room progression
- New player starts in Old Back Room.
- Match outcomes award deterministic, testable XP and coins.
- Purchased upgrades persist across reloads.
- Players cannot spend more coins than they have or unlock gated upgrades early.
- Room can be navigated on touchscreen and pool matches remain playable.
- Existing game remains available until a new version passes tests.

Budget: £0. This file is a design specification, not a claim that these features exist in the game.
