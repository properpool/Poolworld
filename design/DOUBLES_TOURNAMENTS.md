# Pool World — Doubles Tournaments

Status: Player-selected idea for the future Community Choice feature. **Not a live community vote and not implemented.**

## Concept
Let two players team up against another pair in friendly and competitive 2v2 pool tournaments. Encourage cooperation, sportsmanship and community friendships.

## Proposed experience
- Invite a friend or find a teammate through fair matchmaking.
- Alternate turns between teammates according to a clearly published doubles ruleset, with practice tutorials.
- Enter casual matches, pub leagues and scheduled tournaments.
- Team identity, shared trophies and optional cosmetic celebrations.
- Quick, accessible communication tools and safe chat controls.

## Fair-play requirements
- Server-authoritative turn order, fouls, shot validation and match results.
- Equal physics and equipment performance; no pay-to-win.
- Handle disconnects, forfeits and inactive players fairly.
- Report/block controls, moderation and anti-collusion protections.

## Priority and dependencies
Keep on the feature ideas roadmap for community consideration after core 8-ball gameplay, reliable online multiplayer, accounts and anti-cheat protections are implemented. No release date promised.
