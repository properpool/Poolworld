# Pool World — Community Ideas Board

Status: Approved product requirement; **not implemented**.

## Purpose
Give players a welcoming, accessible way to suggest new game features and help prioritise the roadmap without compromising fair play or safety.

## Proposed in-game experience
- A clearly labelled **Community Ideas** menu in the sports bar and main menu.
- Submit a short idea with category: Pool Gameplay, Social & Friends, Venues & Rooms, Events, Accessibility, Bugs, Other.
- Browse suggestions with search and category filters.
- Support ideas with one vote per account per idea; allow users to withdraw their vote.
- Show status: New, Under Review, Planned, In Development, Released, Not Planned, with plain-language explanations.
- Monthly **You Suggested, We Built** update crediting contributors only with their permission.
- Optional polls to compare shortlisted ideas, never a promise that highest vote always wins.

## Community safety and quality
- Rate-limit submissions and votes; prevent duplicate voting and obvious spam.
- Report, mute and block abuse; moderate content before public display as needed.
- No personal information, harassment or copyright-infringing submissions.
- No payment to submit, vote or gain influence. No pay-to-win ideas.
- Clear notice that suggestions are nonbinding; avoid promises of rewards or ownership transfers without a separately reviewed policy.
- Consider privacy, age-appropriate participation, accessibility and moderation staffing before enabling public submissions.

## Launch approach
1. Keep this as a tracked design requirement while stabilising core pool gameplay.
2. Prototype an offline, clearly labelled demonstration of the idea board.
3. Build account-based submissions and voting only with secure backend, moderation and abuse protections.
4. Publish a visible changelog and community update cadence when operationally sustainable.

## Acceptance criteria
- Users can discover and understand the feature.
- One authenticated account cannot inflate an idea's vote count.
- Reports and moderation actions work.
- Suggestion statuses are truthful and updated.
- Ideas are not lost across sessions or devices once the online service launches.
