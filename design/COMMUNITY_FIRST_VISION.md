# Pool World — Community-first vision

Status: Agreed design direction; not yet implemented.

## Product promise
Pool World is a high-quality competitive pool game with a welcoming social community built around it. Players should want to return to their favourite virtual pub to play, meet regulars and catch up with friends. Quick Play must always allow immediate access to pool without mandatory social navigation.

## Social pillars
1. Favourite virtual pub with familiar regulars and a comfortable atmosphere.
2. Friends, invitations, friendly challenges and visits to player-owned games rooms.
3. Pool teams and weekly pub leagues that reward participation and sportsmanship.
4. Spectating and cheering for friends, with controls against harassment.
5. Virtual drinks and celebrations: standard gifts with earned coins, optional clearly priced cosmetic purchases later, no competitive advantage.
6. Community reputation based on positive, verified conduct, never purchases or popularity alone.

## Safety and inclusion
- Moderated chat, report/block/mute, spam prevention, privacy controls and age-appropriate access before public chat launches.
- Do not expose precise location, real names or contact details by default.
- No pay-to-win, no cash-out gifts, and no pressure to spend.
- Fair matchmaking and beginner-friendly activities; optional social participation.

## Delivery order
First stabilise core pool physics, rules, controls and full matches. Then add progression and offline social venues with NPCs. Build secure multiplayer, chat, gifting, spectating and leagues only after server-side validation, moderation and performance testing.

## Success measures (targets, not observed results)
- Match completion and crash-free play.
- Returning players and repeat matches.
- Friend invitations and positive social interactions.
- Low rates of harassment, spam and confirmed cheating.
- Players can enjoy the game without spending money.
