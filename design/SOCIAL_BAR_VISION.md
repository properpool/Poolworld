# Pool World — Social Bar vision

Status: Concept approved in discussion; not implemented.

## Player experience
Players can walk around a virtual pub/sports bar, sit near the counter, meet other players, challenge them to pool, watch matches, and give each other virtual drinks as friendly gestures. Drinks include virtual pints, soft drinks, coffee and mocktails. All items are cosmetic, with no actual alcohol and no competitive advantage.

## Proposed features
- In-game chat at the bar; player profiles, friends and match invitations.
- Gift a virtual drink or round using earned in-game coins; recipients may accept or decline.
- Optional emotes and celebratory animations.
- NPC patrons in offline single-player venues before live social multiplayer.
- Later: visits to player-owned games rooms and public sports bars.

## Safety and product constraints
- Block, mute and report controls; spam/rate limits and chat moderation.
- Privacy settings; avoid exposing personal information or precise location.
- Consider age-appropriate social access and parental safeguards before launch.
- Never require real-money spending to participate in social gameplay.
- Gifts cannot be cashed out or traded for real money; no gambling mechanics.
- £0 development budget until explicitly changed.

## Sequencing
First stabilise core pool gameplay and persistent progression; then create an offline NPC social bar prototype; add real-time chat/gifting only with tested moderation and suitable infrastructure.
