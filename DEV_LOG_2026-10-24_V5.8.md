## Actual engine changes
1. Replaced arbitrary ball-to-ball tangential friction with a rigid-sphere contact model: normal restitution 0.955, tangent impulse based on relative surface slip including both balls' spin, moment-of-inertia factor 2/5, and an 8% Coulomb friction cap. Off-centre collisions now impart rotational spin to object balls; English creates signed object-ball throw and opposite-spin transfer. Model is approximate, not calibrated from a real pool table.
2. Follow/draw kick uses the cue ball's incoming travel direction and is bounded by the released rotational-energy reservoir; it cannot inject untracked kinetic energy. Neutral head-on collisions remain straight.
3. Fixed exact-coincident ball centres: choose a stable collision normal from relative velocity and separate fully rather than leaving overlapped balls stuck.
4. Preserved four approved v3.9 sound payloads byte-for-byte and kept v5.7 precision touch aiming and pullback controls intact. No monetisation or online features.
