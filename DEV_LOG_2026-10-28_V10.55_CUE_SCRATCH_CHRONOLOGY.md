# Pool World v10.55 — cue scratch versus object-contact chronology
Date: 28 October 2026 (UTC)
Branch: `development/poolworld-match-engine`
Production: NOT MODIFIED

## User-visible physics defect
A cue ball moving into a side-pocket well could strike an object ball earlier in the **same 120Hz physics step**. The pocket sweep processed cue-ball pocketing before object collisions and, unlike the object-ball case fixed in v10.54, did not defer cue pocketing for an earlier swept collision. The cue vanished and the struck object received **zero momentum** even though the contact preceded the scratch. A scratch remains a foul; the defect was the missing physical collision and lost first-hit chronology, not the ball-in-hand ruling.
