## Gameplay correction

The solver exits early once residual penetration is below 0.01 table units, so ordinary sparse contacts do not incur the full bound. No new velocity, spin, sound, scoring or pocket-capture impulses were introduced. This is a convergence correction, not measured real billiard physics.
