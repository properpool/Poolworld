# Pool World v10.28 — pocket-well sink animation (27 October 2026)

## Reproducible visual defect
A legally captured cue or object ball vanished immediately from the canvas in the same 120 Hz physics tick as the pocket event. The abrupt disappearance made otherwise smooth motion look clipped at the six pockets. This was a moving-ball presentation defect, not evidence that shot impulse or ball collision physics was incorrect.

## Isolated implementation
- `pocketBall` immediately commits `potted`, score and sound exactly as before, and snapshots a separate visual-only ball with the nearest of the six pocket wells.
- The renderer animates the captured sphere over 190 ms using a bounded ease-out translation, scale and alpha. The ghost is never included in physics, collision, rail, turn, cue-ball placement or scoring arrays.
- `frame` continues redrawing the sink after match settlement and clears it at completion; `rack` and `restore` retire old ghosts.
- Minimal physics fixture balls lacking a roll quaternion retain a safe identity-orientation fallback.
