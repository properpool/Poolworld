# Pool World v10.29 — cue-ball scratch fade compositing

Date: 27 October 2026 UTC. Development branch only.

## Reproduced moving-ball visual defect

The v10.28 pocket-sink animation correctly faded a pocketed cue-ball shell, but `drawBall` assigned absolute `ctx.globalAlpha` values to the six red measle dots, ignoring the parent sink fade. It also reset the incoming opacity to 1. As a result, a scratched cue ball could leave unnaturally bright red markers floating in the dark pocket as the porcelain shell disappeared. This is a reproducible rendering defect, **not** a measured physical-device performance issue or a correction to the user's weak shot impulse/jerkiness.
