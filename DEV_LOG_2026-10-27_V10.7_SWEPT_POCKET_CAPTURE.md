# Pool World v10.7 — swept six-pocket capture (27 October 2026)

## Scope and release controls
Development branch only: `development/poolworld-match-engine`. Production `main/index.html` must remain untouched. The physical Samsung Galaxy A37 and independent human acceptance remain release-blocking; browser simulation cannot establish real device smoothness or acceptance.

## User-visible defect
The 120 Hz physics loop previously checked pocket capture only at the *end* of each tick. A ball crossing a shallow chord of a circular pocket well can enter and leave the well within one tick, with both endpoints outside the capture radius. Such a legitimate edge-of-pocket pot was missed, leaving the ball in play. This is analogous to the swept ball-to-ball contact defect corrected earlier.

## Regression
Mandatory `tests/test_pool60_swept_pocket.py` exercises all six pocket wells on 390x844, 844x390, 768x1024 and 1366x768 Chromium viewports. Fixtures cover shallow swept entries with outside endpoints, adjacent near-misses, finite positions, single pocket events, and cue-ball scratch adjudication. It uses the actual `physics(STEP)`, not a copied algorithm. Registered in `.github/workflows/poolworld-staging-tests.yml`.

## Candidate change
Staging-only `POOL_WORLD_6.0_STAGING_MATCH.html` replaces endpoint-only circular capture with minimum squared distance from each ball's swept centre segment to each pocket centre, preserving the exact `POCKET_CAPTURE_RADIUS2` threshold. A conservative axis-aligned rejection skips distant pockets. The existing `pocketBall` path continues to handle sound, cue scratches and match state. Shot calibration, rails, jaws, visuals, rules and the original four audio URIs are otherwise unchanged.
