## Reproduced problem
The existing 120Hz physics loop clamped elapsed animation time to 83.3ms and consumed up to ten physics ticks per callback. A transient 150ms rAF gap silently discarded ~66.7ms of elapsed shot time and could create a ten-tick CPU burst in the next render callback. The new mandatory `tests/test_pool60_hitch_recovery.py` was introduced in commit `bc8e83e37b4ec287fc5504f4edbdbf13471c6907`, registered in the CI workflow at `dce046b2e827ebd26582449ba82c6e5325be2737`, and expanded with idle-resume protection at `d4512cafc6789ed53374430f5971f752f668c9ba`.

The pre-fix CI run #275 (40359781896) recorded **19 physics ticks** across a 233.33ms test window, versus ~28 expected: nine 120Hz ticks of shot movement were lost. The hitch callback ran ten ticks, exceeding the new eight-tick budget. Four genuine hitch-related assertions failed in the first 390x844 viewport; a test-harness JSON-key mistake then stopped the remaining viewports. Run #274 (40359665529) used the earlier test revision and had not completed at documentation time. Do not interpret the harness error as a gameplay regression.

## Gameplay fix
Staging candidate commit `2b4edc8aa12ebd86fd46e0f3d286fbbab3062ef7`, blob `538b6da26f010b9c1aced3aa1f993e622ad5455c`: preserve up to 250ms of elapsed active-shot time and retain outstanding 120Hz simulation debt across successive rAF callbacks, while processing **at most eight physics ticks per callback**. Clear the accumulator while idle so resuming the tab cannot fast-forward a subsequent shot. This is bounded catch-up, not unlimited simulation. All collision, cue-power, pockets, match rules, ball visuals and four approved audio data URIs remain unchanged.

## Limitations and next priority
The measured fix prevents *synthetic* elapsed-shot time loss after transient browser stalls; it is not evidence of 60fps or eliminated weak/jerky shots on the Samsung Galaxy A37. Continue to profile actual active full-rack requestAnimationFrame p95/p99 spikes, garbage collection and real pointer-event-to-visible-canvas timing. Reproduce any user-visible defect with mandatory regression before altering gameplay. Do not add multiplayer, monetisation, marketing or unrelated expansion.
