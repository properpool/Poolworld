# Pool World v8.1 — post-contact pocket-jaw clearance

Date: 2026-10-26 UTC. Branch: `development/poolworld-match-engine`.
Scope: six-pocket rubber jaw geometry, object-ball impacts and constrained positional separation only.

## Reproduced gameplay defect
The existing physics resolved jaw collisions before object-ball impulses. An incoming ball could then drive another ball *into* a rubber jaw after that jaw pass. The post-contact rail and ball-ball correction did not guard against the twelve jaw noses, leaving a visibly intersecting ball for one 120Hz tick.

New mandatory `tests/test_pool60_post_contact_jaw.py` (commit `ae4df4c13553e309c9c0952dec000986db84528a`) tests two-ball impacts aimed at each of the twelve jaw noses at two velocities across four Chromium viewports (390×844, 844×390, 768×1024, 1366×768). Each fixture checks immediate and 24-tick jaw clearance, legal cloth bounds, no false pocketing and finite, non-energy-creating motion. Total: 484 checks. Workflow registration commit: `bcb3d73bb21933d81b8d84cffb834e9cdbf48316`.

Baseline workflow #192, run `40250216413`, FAILED: 3,563/3,659 total checks, including 388/484 new checks. The 96 failures were genuine immediate jaw penetrations, e.g. a ball centre 17.618346 table units from a jaw requiring at least 19.2 units. Baseline #191 (before the workflow registration) succeeded on the previous 3,175 checks.
