# Pool World v10.54 — object/object collision before pocket capture

Date: 28 October 2026 (UTC). Development branch: `development/poolworld-match-engine`.
Scope: mandatory gameplay physics correction; no production deployment.

## Reproduced user-visible defect

The 120Hz physics loop integrates and pocket-captures each ball before
resolving object/object contacts. Earlier work deferred a pocket capture when
the cue ball contacted an object first, but a **different object ball** could
still collide with that object earlier in the same tick and lose its entire
impulse. The struck object vanished into the well before collision resolution,
while the incoming ball kept almost all its speed. This makes multi-ball
shots near a side pocket look physically wrong.

In a representative 390x844 Chromium fixture (top side pocket, 75% calibration),
an object moving toward the well at speed 8 was hit by a following object
moving at speed 14 *before* the well-entry time. The baseline incorrectly
pocketed the first ball immediately and left the following ball moving at
speed 13.9908, with no ball-impact sound.

## Fix

At a swept pocket entry, compare the analytically determined pocket-entry
fraction against potential earlier swept ball/ball contact fractions. For
later-processed balls, predict the same friction-adjusted displacement
without changing their state. Do not defer for a collision occurring after
pocket entry, a lateral near-miss, or a striker that would itself be pocketed
before contact. Resolve an earlier real impact first, then let the existing
post-contact pocket pass capture the ball and record scoring, sound and sink
visual exactly once.

Preserves existing calibrated `shotSpeed`, 120Hz physics step, six pocket
geometry, cue first-hit rules, complete match handling, graphics and the
four original approved v3.9 audio data URI bytes.
