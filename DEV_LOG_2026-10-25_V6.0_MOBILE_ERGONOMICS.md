# Pool World v6.0 — mobile controls and landscape playability candidate

Date: 25 October 2026 (scheduled development run, after corner-jaw calibration)
Branch: `development/poolworld-match-engine` in `properpool/Poolworld`.

## Problem addressed
Actual player reported the previous v5.9 cue ball barely moved and balls would not pot. v6.0 power and pocket calibration already addressed the measured break defect, but a fresh real-browser screenshot at 844×390 showed **SHOOT was only 57×25 CSS pixels** and the landscape power track **30px wide** with a **32px handle clipped by the track**. These were poor touch targets for a Samsung phone. Table occupied only 470.9×244.0 CSS pixels, leaving usable screen area wasted.

## Changes made (candidate, NOT promoted to staging index)
- Increased compact-landscape SHOOT button to **76×44px** and power track to **44px wide**, with a 42px visible handle. Portrait SHOOT now at least **80×44px** and aim arrows at least 42px tall.
- Enlarged 844×390 landscape table from **470.9×244.0** to **507.6×263.0px** (+16.2% visible table area). Preserved the canvas's original 1100×570 aspect ratio and physics coordinates.
- Kept status text visible and moved collapsed shot-adjustments into a bottom-right floating control; opening the control exposes functional power, side-spin and top/backspin sliders.
- On very narrow landscape screens (568×320), the controls form a single horizontally scrollable row instead of wrapping below the viewport; tested access to the GUIDE control after scrolling.
- Did not change ball physics, power curve, pocket jaws, match rules or the four approved v3.9 audio payloads.
