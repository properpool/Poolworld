Date: 25 October 2026. Branch: `development/poolworld-match-engine`, repo `properpool/Poolworld`.

## Change
All sixteen balls now integrate a visual unit quaternion for rolling displacement/radius. Striped object-ball bands are attached to their rotating sphere orientation, so stripes rotate and foreshorten rather than remaining fixed. Number badges remain upright for gameplay legibility. This is visual rolling, not a new physically calibrated angular momentum model. Shot power, cushion/jaw physics, match rules and approved v3.9 audio payloads are unchanged.
