# Pool World v10.22 — Cue/ball impact synchronisation
Date: 27 October 2026 (UTC)
Scope: `development/poolworld-match-engine` only. Production `main/index.html` is not authorised for modification.

## Reproduced user-visible defect
The physical cue-ball impulse is applied immediately by `shoot()`, but the visible cue-stick animation previously began in its fully retracted position and advanced toward the stationary strike point for 65% of a 160ms animation. That is **104ms after the cue ball has already started moving**. On the first shot frame, cue tips at 15%, 45% and 90% power were respectively -44.9, -56.3 and -73.4 table units from the original cue-ball centre, rather than near the contact offset of -16.2. The stick appeared to hit a ball that had already left its starting position. This is a visual timing defect, not evidence of incorrect physical impulse.

## Fix and preserved invariants

The power-dependent pre-shot cue pullback remains visible during aiming. The authoritative cue impulse is **not delayed**, and no ball velocity, friction, collisions, six-pocket geometry, match rules, input mappings or approved sound recording URI bytes were changed.
